FAQ

Questions players ask.

What is BLOX?

The first open-world trenches in GTA: a live voxel city where the Solana trenches play out in real time. Every pump.fun coin walks the streets as a character, perps sail the harbor and pump.fun whales fly overhead. Ape in on a coin (buy it through Jupiter) in one signature, set limit orders, hedge with perps on Hyperliquid, take missions, follow the most wanted traders and park your bag in your own safehouse, all from one screen.

What is the city made of?

Live data, nothing staged. Every pump.fun coin walks the streets as a character with its logo for a head: gold for new pairs seconds old, blue for coins that just graduated to PumpSwap, green when up and pink when down. The busier a coin, the taller it stands and the faster it walks, and every green or pink pop above one is a trade at its real 5-minute pace. The cars are traffic, the police, and you. The bubbles over the city are real events as they happen (a launch, a migration, a coin about to bond) and crypto topics trending on X right now. The boats in the harbor are the biggest Hyperliquid perps, the helicopters carry pump.fun whales (Cupsey and Cented) and every trade they make pops up live, the billboards show the biggest coins on the streets and the police circle the block of whichever coin is dumping hardest. Every pump.fun launch adds a floor to Vice Tower downtown, every graduation you witness takes off from the airport with its ticker, a plane tows the #1 trending coin along the beach, and the arena's screen keeps the live score. When a feed is down, that part of the city goes quiet instead of making things up.

What do the mood meter, fireworks and rain mean?

The mood meter is the share of the last 5 minutes of trading that went into coins that are up. When the trenches are green, pumping coins fire off fireworks and the sky warms up; when they turn red there are more explosions and lightning, the sky darkens and below about 38% green it starts to rain.

What are missions and respect?

Missions are live calls from the feeds: the biggest pump, the biggest dump, the coin the cops are chasing, the busiest coin, a new drop, a big perp move, extreme funding and the whale of the day. Open them from the phone or press M, and tap one to jump to it. Every swap or hedge you finish here counts as a job, and jobs move you up the ranks from Rookie to Legend of Vice. Respect is kept in this browser only.

What is the safehouse?

Connect a Solana wallet and your bag stands in the safehouse yard: one character per coin you hold (up to ten), with its value, plus a beam of light so you can find it. Tap one to cash out, hedge or open its chart. Nothing moves without your signature.

How do I get around?

You're in a car. Drive with WASD or the arrow keys, Shift to boost, Space to brake; on a phone, use the joystick. Press V (or the Map button) for the map view, where you drag to look around and scroll or pinch to zoom; press V again to get back in the car. Tap any coin character, boat, billboard or helicopter to open its card. The phone (bottom right, or P) holds every terminal page as an app.

Is BLOX part of GTA, Rockstar or Take-Two?

No. BLOX is a fan-made memecoin and trading terminal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. GTA is their trademark.

Does BLOX hold my money?

No. Your SOL and coins stay in your Solana wallet and every swap is signed there. Your margin stays in your own Hyperliquid account. There is no BLOX account and no deposit to us.

How do trades work?

Swaps go through Jupiter, which finds the best route across Solana, pump.fun curves included. You see the quote, your wallet signs once, and Jupiter sends it. Perps go straight to Hyperliquid.

How do limit orders work?

Pick Limit in the buy or sell box and set your price. Jupiter keeps the order onchain and fills it when the coin trades at your price, even with this tab closed. Cancel any time and the funds come back to your wallet. Jupiter's minimum is about $5 per order.

What is the Whales page?

The public Hyperliquid leaderboard, ranked by the window you pick, plus every open position of any trader on it. Copy or fade opens the same perp in the terminal; you choose the size and sign it yourself. You can also watch any Solana wallet, priced live. It's read-only: nothing is signed and nothing is stored.

What is the session key?

Hyperliquid lets you approve an "agent" key that can place and cancel orders for your account but can never withdraw. BLOX creates one in your browser, your wallet approves it once, and it stays in this browser only. Forget it any time from the wallet menu.

What does hedging actually do?

A short gains when its price falls. If your coin and the perp fall together, the short pays back part of what the bag lost. If your coin pumps while the perp is flat, you keep most of the pump. The hedge costs you some upside and pays or earns funding.

My coin has no perp. Can I still hedge?

Yes, with SOL or another meme perp as a market hedge. It covers the "whole market dumps" risk, not the "this coin rugs" risk. The terminal says which one you're getting.

Which wallets work?

For memes: any Solana wallet that supports Wallet Standard, like Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. For perps: any EVM wallet, like Phantom, MetaMask or Rabby, with USDC on Hyperliquid.

What does it cost?

Jupiter's swap fee and Hyperliquid's trading fee, the same as on their own sites. BLOX adds nothing.

What can go wrong?

Memecoins can go to zero. Leverage can be liquidated if the perp pumps far enough. Funding can turn against a short. A market hedge can fall less than your coin. Only trade what you can lose.
